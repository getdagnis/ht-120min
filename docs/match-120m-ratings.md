# Finished-match 120mRt

The current-season fixture badge rates each team's preparation for a 120-minute friendly. It does not use goals, match duration, or opponent strength. `shared/match-120m-rating.ts` owns formula version 16 and the score-to-grade bands. Raw CHPP sector ratings use the same `(raw + 3) / 4` display conversion as the fixture card.

Formation advice treats 4-5-1 as the best practical balance for midfield training and regular matches. Suggestions to use 5-5-0 or 5-4-1 are conditional. 4-5-1 and 5-4-1 both have 95 formation points in the current formula, so switching between them alone does not guarantee a higher score.

The score adds defence points at 30%, tactic points at 25%, formation points up to 18, and midfield-balance points at 20%. Set pieces add `max(0, 25 − 2 × set-piece skill)` points, so a weaker taker earns more 120m credit and the credit reaches zero at skill 13; missing or unknown skill gets neutral credit of 12.5 points. The positive subtotal is capped at 100 before SE and attack penalties. Defence compares average defence with average attack. Midfield balance compares average defence with midfield plus average attack. A formation's missing points are protected in proportion to the weighted defence and midfield-balance points. The protection fraction is `(30 × defence points + 20 × midfield points) / 5000`, capped from 0 to 1; the division by 100 converts the weighted 0–100 component scores into a fraction. This lets strong underlying ratings offset part of an attacking formation penalty. Pressing earns 25 points at tactic skill 15 or higher and loses 1.5 points per level below 15. The strongest attack sector, SE-threat specialties, and formation points not protected by defence and midfield lower the score. The hidden score adjustment subtracts one point for each danger-colored formation, tactic, and set-piece indicator; one extra point for an SE threat penalty of 7–9, two for 10–14, or three for 15 or higher; and one point if any pitch position is F-red. A fully defensive coach mindset adds no bonus. Defensive sectors receive a B-color when every sector is at least 5 and average defence is at least 1.5 times both the highest attack sector and midfield. They receive the A-color when every sector is at least 5 and average defence is at least twice the highest attack sector and 1.5 times midfield, or when all three sectors are at least 11 and average defence is at least 12. Weak sectors below 5 remain F-red. Midfield balance warnings use the same grade colors as the overall badge (below 25/100 is F, 25 to below 40 is E, and 40 to below 55 is D), but color only midfield when its rating exceeds average attack; the tooltip advises lowering attack to preserve midfield. Attack sectors are colored only by their own comparison to average defence or the direct 8 and 11.25 thresholds, with tooltips advising how to preserve attack by lowering midfield or strengthening defence. These warning colors describe component severity and do not alter the weighted overall grade. During Pressing, Powerful defenders are marked green because their defending contribution to the tactic is doubled. Powerful midfielders still count double toward Pressing, but their markers stay neutral because they can also create secondary scoring chances. Quick and Head defenders count as 0.5 toward their specialty totals; other outfield roles count as 1. The red specialty marker and the three-player recommendation threshold begin at a weighted total of 3. For the Head SE threat penalty, every lineup position counts fully, including goalkeeper and defenders. The SE-penalty value has a tooltip breaking down weighted role counts and points by specialty. Missing core ratings, formation, or tactic leave a side unscored. Unknown specialties get no penalty. The saved `has_set_pieces_skill` and `has_specialties` flags preserve those distinctions without changing the badge.

At an SE penalty grade of C or lower, every specialty marker on a position that contributes to the threat penalty uses that grade's color, including contributing defenders. At A or B, the existing weighted-count warning remains. The penalty tooltip lists only specialties with nonzero points and displays numbers without redundant trailing zeros.

`migrations/139_match_120m_ratings.sql` adds one row per fixture side with the actual participating team's UUID and Hattrick ID, score, grade, formula version, and input-availability flags. Historical snapshots and standings are unchanged. Rows survive a later team-row deletion. Public users may read rows; only the service role may replace them. The replacement RPC locks the match, checks the saved performance snapshot, current-season mode, and actual participant IDs, then replaces both sides in one transaction. A match-update trigger removes stale rows when performance, team identity, round, or completion changes. This also covers result reset paths that do not calculate a new score. Pre-kickoff predicted ratings remain separate and private.

Live MatchDetails refresh, manual match linking/addition, and the authorized match-facts refresh calculate scores after saving finished facts. The current-season public fixture projection reads stored rows; page visits do no rating calculation or CHPP lookup. `scripts/backfill-match-120m-ratings.ts` scans current-season finished fixtures from stored performance only, paginates reads, and defaults to a read-only dry run. Pass `--apply` to write via the guarded RPC. It never fetches CHPP and leaves historical fixture snapshots unchanged. Future ranking tables can aggregate rows by `team_id` and `formula_version`.

Score preparation resolves tournament ownership through the fixture's round. It uses each saved performance side's Hattrick team ID to keep the score with the correct scheduled fixture participant, including reversed venues and reserve substitutions. An ambiguous identity stays unscored. Automatic scoring failures are logged and reported separately from saved results so they cannot prevent length-round progression.

Rollout order: apply migration 139 after preceding prepared migrations, deploy the code, inspect a dry run, then explicitly run the backfill if the owner chooses. Localhost may use production Supabase, so even the backfill dry run reads live data; its apply mode writes live data. Formula changes need a new version and deliberate recalculation, not silent reinterpretation of old scores.

```sh
node --env-file-if-exists=.env --env-file-if-exists=.env.local --import tsx scripts/backfill-match-120m-ratings.ts --tournament-id UUID
# After reviewing the dry-run count and applying migration 139:
node --env-file-if-exists=.env --env-file-if-exists=.env.local --import tsx scripts/backfill-match-120m-ratings.ts --tournament-id UUID --apply
```

Formula version 15 adds a dedicated 10-point counter-attacks deduction, beyond its existing zero tactic credit and indicator penalty. Predicted and completed-match calculations share this rule and a specific tactic tooltip. Stored grades are not automatically recalculated; any backfill remains separately authorized.

Formula version 16 applies the same additional 10-point deduction to Play Creatively and Long Shots. Their zero tactic credit and indicator penalty still apply. The coaching recommendation impact and predicted/completed tactic tooltips include the deduction. Existing stored grades remain at their saved formula version until explicitly recalculated.
